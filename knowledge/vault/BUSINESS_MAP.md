# Business map

BASELINE: consult exact process contracts in specification/v1.0.md through the ICM/spec commands.

| Node | Business model | Keep distinct |
| --- | --- | --- |
| manga | Independent creators and licensed publisher editions feed one free ad-supported reader | Reading activity versus settled ad earnings, creator versus publisher economics, original grant/edition identities |
| anime | Existing anime licensing and one accountable external prime producer feed one paid subscription streamer | Streaming rights versus media QC, commission stage versus earned obligation, entitlement versus confirmed cash |
| merch | Authorized wholesale and one accountable external lead-manufacturer production feed one accepted-stock storefront | Custody, inspection, ownership, sellability, classified lot cost, unpaid holds, paid allocation, cash and refunds |
| owner | Company governance, exact decisions, departmental export permissions and central BI | Not a fourth selling department; analytics never grant operational authority |

Rights, feasibility, Finance, acceptance and actual provider evidence are separate. Preserve original native source identities, scope/version/date/currency/units and audit history. Financial corrections are linked adjustments, not rewritten originals. Missing contracts/amounts/ownership/classification hold downstream actions; do not fill zero.

A cross-department opportunity/referral does not itself authorize adaptation, disclosure, spending or publication. Put explicit counterpart request/response and acknowledgement contracts in contracts/ before transport implementation.
